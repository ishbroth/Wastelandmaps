AGE SUITABILITY

WastelandMaps, an unofficial fan project. What is in the game, so you can decide whether it suits you or your child.
In short: WastelandMaps is meant for ages 12 and up. It is a map that shows real places as a post-apocalyptic wasteland, with a cartoon-style shooting game against mutated creatures, robots and raiders. There is no blood and gore, no sexual content, no gambling, no ads, and no chat with strangers.

WHAT IS IN THE GAME

  • Cartoon-style violence. The shooting gallery has you fire guns and explosives at animated creatures (giant insects, mole rats, scorpions, mutants), robots and human raiders. Enemies fall or disappear; there is no realistic injury, dismemberment or gore. A brief red tint at the screen's edge shows that you were hit.
  • Post-apocalyptic themes. Destroyed buildings, radiation, flooded coasts, mutants and ruins. Real landmarks are shown as ruined, which some younger children may find unsettling.
  • Real-world places. Places on the map are real. Snapshots of landmarks are made from public photos. A player can also turn a street address into a ruined picture, made from Google Street View imagery; a picture made for an address is shared with other players, so please do not render an address you would not want others to see.
  • References to alcohol and medicine. Some in-game items and place types (bars, pharmacies, "chems" in the loot names) are mentioned by name. Nothing is depicted being used.
  • Location. The app uses the phone's location to show where you are and to discover places as you reach them. See the privacy policy. Please do not walk or drive while looking at your phone; the game never needs you to go anywhere unsafe.

WHAT IS NOT IN THE GAME

  • No blood and gore, no sexual or suggestive content, no strong language.
  • No gambling and no loot boxes. Loot comes from clearing places and is not bought with chance.
  • No advertising and no tracking for advertising.
  • No chat, messaging or user-to-user contact. The leaderboard shows only made-up callsigns, never a name, email or address.
  • No way to share photos or write anything other people can read.

PURCHASES

Bottle caps can be bought, in packs from a few dollars, to unlock snapshots of places. Purchases are optional and the game can be played without buying anything. In the iPhone app, purchases are charged to the Apple account, so a parent can control them with Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time settings.

FOR PARENTS

  • Children under 13 should have a parent's permission to use an account. If you believe a child has made an account without it, write to us and we will delete it.
  • Your child can leave the leaderboard, or delete the account entirely, from the Data tab.
  • To turn off purchases on an iPhone, use Settings, Screen Time, Content & Privacy Restrictions, iTunes & App Store Purchases.
