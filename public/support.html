SUPPORT

WastelandMaps, an unofficial fan project. Answers to the common questions, and how to reach us.
Need a hand? Write to support@wastelandmaps.com. Tell us what device you use (iPhone, Android or the website), what you tried, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

GETTING STARTED

Do I need an account?

You can look around the map without one. Sign in to keep what you do: the places you find, your snapshots, loot, guns, caps, levels and your home vault follow you to any device.

How do I sign in?

Enter your email and the four-digit vault number you chose for your home. The first time, or if you forgot the number, choose EMAIL ME A LINK and open the link on the same device. There is no other password.

What is my home vault?

When you first sign in you pick where home is and a four-digit vault number, 0000 to 9999 (it may start with a zero, and 0730 is not the same as Vault 730). Your home becomes your own vault on the map, and no one else can have your number. It is also your sign-in number. You can change your home from the Data tab.

I forgot my vault number.

On the sign-in screen enter your email and choose EMAIL ME A LINK INSTEAD. Once you are in, your number is shown in the HOME section of the Data tab.

NAVIGATION

How do I start navigating to a place?

Tap a place on the map, then tap its travel button. The navigation needs your real position, so AUTO-DISCOVER must be switched on and the app must be allowed to use your location. It draws the route as a glowing path with pulsing arrows, shows the next turn and the distance in the bar at the top, and shows the distance and time left along the bottom. Tap END to stop.

Can it speak the directions? Does it work in my car?

Yes. In the app, directions are spoken in a robot voice. They play through whatever the phone is connected to, including car Bluetooth or CarPlay audio and headphones, lower other audio while they speak, and keep going with the screen locked. Use VOICE ON / VOICE OFF in the navigation bar to switch it. The full map is not shown on the car's screen; it stays on the phone.

The navigation says it could not get directions.

The route service could not be reached. Check your connection and try again in a moment. If the place is across the water, the app offers a link to flights instead.

I left the route. What happens?

If you drift more than a short way off the route, the app works out a new one and says "Recalculating route."

The map is slow or my battery drains quickly.

Navigation and AUTO-DISCOVER use location, the screen and the network. Turn AUTO-DISCOVER off when you do not need it, and end navigation when you arrive. Zooming out also shows fewer icons, which keeps the map lighter.

DISCOVERING PLACES AND LEVELLING UP

How do I discover a place?

Switch AUTO-DISCOVER on and go there. When you are within about a block or two (roughly 120 to 250 meters, depending on how accurate your location is) the place is discovered: its icon fills in, you hear a chirp and you earn XP. Arriving by navigation discovers it too. It keeps working with the phone in your pocket or locked.

How much XP is a place worth?

It depends on the place. An ordinary place is 10 points, a bridge, school or hospital 15, a library, theater, stadium or restaurant 20, a government building, museum or skyscraper 30, a famous landmark 50 and a landmark with a free snapshot 75. A place only counts once.

How do levels work?

Level 1 takes 80 points, and every level after that asks for 5% more than the one before. Each level you reach pays you 100 bottle caps. Level rewards are limited to two levels' worth (200 caps) in any one day.

Why did a place not get discovered?

Check that AUTO-DISCOVER is on, that location is allowed for the app (always, if you want it to work in your pocket) and that you were close enough. Places you reach are checked against your real position, so spoofed locations do not count.

What does [CLEARED] under a city's name mean?

You have looted every building and landmark in that city that counts (the ones with a photograph, so a snapshot can always be unlocked). You get a 400-caps bonus, once for each city. See your progress for each city in the METROS CLEARED list on the Data tab.

UNLOCKING SNAPSHOTS

What is a snapshot?

A snapshot is the real photograph of a place torn apart by a blast to reveal it as it looks two hundred years after the bombs. You can replay the animation, save the picture or the video, and share them.

What does a snapshot cost?

Landmarks with a free snapshot cost nothing. A famous landmark or any place with a free photograph on the map is 100 caps. A street-level snapshot of any address, including your own home, is 400 caps. The unlock button shows the price and your balance, for example 400/5,200.

A snapshot is not available for a place.

Some places have no free photograph, and some addresses have no street imagery. In that case the snapshot tab says so and nothing is charged. If a snapshot cannot be made, or something goes wrong while it is being made, your caps are returned automatically.

What happens to the picture of my address?

A street-level render is part of the public atlas of destroyed places: everyone who unlocks the same address sees the same picture, and it appears on a page of this site that search engines can find. Your name and email are never attached to it. The original street photo is shown straight from Google and is not stored by us. Your own home vault render is the exception: it carries your vault number and stays private to you.

What is the address atlas for a city?

The Data tab can unlock every address that other survivors have rendered in the city on the map, now and in the future, for 4,800 caps. They then appear on your map.

GAMEPLAY

How do I loot a place?

Open a place whose snapshot is unlocked and press LOOT. You fight what lives there: tap or hold the screen to shoot, and use the WEAPONS button to switch guns (the fight pauses while you choose). Win and you are paid loot. Each place can be looted once.

The LOOT button is faded. Why?

You have to discover a place before you can loot it. Go there, or navigate there, and come back. The exceptions are your own home vault, which you can loot as soon as its snapshot is unlocked, and the landmarks with a free snapshot.

I fought a landmark from afar. Where is my loot?

You can fight a landmark with a free snapshot before you have been there, but the loot waits until you actually discover it. The moment you do, it is delivered automatically and you get a notification.

How do I die, and what are strikes?

Enemies strike when they reach you, and you can take three strikes. The third is fatal. If you have a stimpak, the fight stops and asks whether to use it. Declining counts as dying, and you can try again.

What are stimpaks?

A stimpak takes back all your strikes. You make them in the Data tab by trading spare loot, for example 3 wood, 3 screws and 2 adhesive for one. Your count is shown in the fight and on the Data tab.

How much loot do I get, and is there a limit?

The more valuable the place, the more it pays: 2 to 3 pieces for the smallest places, up to 7 to 10 for the landmarks with a free snapshot. You can carry 99 of each kind. Anything more is left behind and the fight tells you.

GUNS

How do the guns work?

There are four classes: pistols, automatic weapons, sniper rifles and explosives. Each has four tiers, every one a real gun. You start with the tier 1 pistol. Open the WEAPONS button during a fight to choose a class; locked classes are not built yet.

How do I get better guns?

Build them in the GUNS section of the Data tab with loot. The first gun of a class costs 4 pieces of loot, tier 2 costs 7 more, tier 3 costs 10 more and tier 4 costs 15 more. Tiers cannot be skipped, and each gun uses its own mix of loot.

Which gun is best?

Automatics fire fast with the button held. Snipers hit very hard but slowly. Pistols are steady and cheap. Explosives hit everything in a blast, and some have limited ammunition for each fight. Bosses and big enemies take more shots, so build up as you go.

BOTTLE CAPS AND PURCHASES

What are bottle caps?

Caps are the wasteland's money. You spend them on snapshots. Your balance is shown in the Data tab, and next to the price when you unlock something.

How do I earn caps?

Reach new levels (100 caps each) and clear cities (400 caps each). Caps you earn this way are free.

How do I buy caps?

When you are short on the unlock button, a window offers three packs: 800 caps for $1.99, 2,400 caps for $4.99 and 5,200 caps for $9.99. In the iPhone and iPad app, purchases are in-app purchases charged to your Apple ID. On the website, you pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through our payment provider.

My purchase did not show up.

It can take up to a minute. Close and reopen the app or reload the page and check the Data tab. If your caps still have not arrived, write to us with the email you signed in with and the receipt.

REFUNDS

I was charged caps and did not get a snapshot.

If a snapshot cannot be made, your caps are returned automatically. If you were charged and nothing was returned, write to us and we will put them back.

How do I get a refund for a purchase?

Bought in the iPhone or iPad app: Apple handles the payment, so refunds go through Apple. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the purchase and choose Request a refund. Apple decides.

Bought on the website: write to us at support@wastelandmaps.com with the email you signed in with. Caps from a recent purchase that you have not spent can normally be refunded. Caps you have already spent on a snapshot cannot.

Do my caps expire?

No. Your caps stay on your account until you spend them.

YOUR ACCOUNT

How do I delete my account?

Open the Data tab, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete my account, then confirm. This permanently removes your account, caps, loot, guns, home and your own places. It cannot be undone.
