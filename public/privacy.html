PRIVACY POLICY

WastelandMaps, an unofficial fan project. Last updated 31 October 2026.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign in. We email you a sign-in link; there is no password.
  • Your location, only if you allow it: to show where you are, to discover places as you reach them, and to give directions. With the app's "AUTO-DISCOVER" switched on, location is also used while the phone is locked or the app is in the background, so places can be found while you walk or drive; the app shows a notification while it does this. Turn AUTO-DISCOVER off, or location off in your phone's settings, to stop. Your location is used to work out which places are near you and is not shown to other people.
  • What you do in the game: places you have found, snapshots you have unlocked, credits, loot and guns, and any addresses you travel to or choose as home. These are tied to your account so they follow you to your other devices.
  • Addresses you render. When you unlock a street snapshot of an address, that address, its coordinates and the picture made from it are kept so other players who unlock the same address see the same picture. Your name or email is not attached to it, and the picture does not show people.
  • Purchases: what you bought and your credit balance. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe (on the website); we never see or store them.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase stores your account and game data.
  • Google provides the maps, place search, directions and Street View. Searches you make and pictures requested are sent to Google.
  • Stripe processes payments on the website.
  • Google's image model (Gemini) creates the snapshot pictures from photographs (including Street View images, which are used once and not kept).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it to track you across other apps or websites. There are no advertising or analytics trackers.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app or on the website: open your account, choose Delete my account, and confirm. This permanently removes your account, credits, loot, guns, found places and personal places. Pictures of addresses already in the shared atlas stay, without anything linking them to you.

Children

WastelandMaps is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: support@wastelandmaps.com.
